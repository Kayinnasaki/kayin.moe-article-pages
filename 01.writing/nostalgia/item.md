Nostalgia is a topic I [circle back to](/nostalgia-vs-having-an-active-relationship-with-your-media) a lot. Even things like my essay on [Remakes](/why-play-a-remake) is, in part, an essay about nostalgia. Anyone who follows me on [social media](https://bsky.app/profile/kayin.moe) has seem me rant about this endless amounts of times. It feels like rehashing old ground, but sometimes I need a chance to put all my thoughts in one place.


I don't hate nostalgia. A song on the radio I haven't thought about since highschool. Sometimes I get hit by the sensation of the pincushion moss on the side of a hill I remember from upstate New York. Nostalgia can be a passing memory, or a reminder that old things you loved still exist.
